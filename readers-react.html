Refugees React

Heartfelt words from Kosovar refugees and former scholarship students who lived through these history-defining events.

"Over my winter break we read your book. My husband and I were excited to see your story unfold our past. There were times that I pictured myself through it, there were times I got emotional while reading it."
— --AC
"I started reading the book and it literally brought me back a quarter of a century! It is eerily realistic, and you seem to have a great job capturing things in a very accurate way."
— --FF
"Congratulations on publishing your book. Your dedication to telling the story of Kosova, the resilience of its people, and the refugee experience means a great deal to me. Those years shaped who we are, and it is powerful to know that these stories are being preserved and shared with those who did not experience them firsthand."
— --LB
"I have never forgotten what you did for me and for so many of us who came to the U.S. as refugees from Kosova in 1999. In those early days, we were trying to survive a new world while carrying the weight of what we had lived through. To have someone like you step in with kindness, effort, and belief in our future… that meant more than I can put into words. You didn’t just help with school - you helped give us a direction when everything felt uncertain. I’m deeply moved that you turned those experiences into a book. The fact that you took the time to return to Kosova, listen, interview, and carry those stories with respect - and then dedicate the book to the people of Kosova - says so much about the kind of person you are. Thank you for making sure our struggles are known by those who weren’t there to witness them."
— --AG
"Thank you for keeping the love and care for Kosovars alive. My family and I deeply appreciate what you and so many Americans have done for Kosovars. We will always be grateful to the United States—and to ambassadors of peace like you."
— --MB

Readers Respond

Verified 5-star reviews from Amazon readers.

"An incredibly powerful and emotional read."

As I went through each page, I felt as though I was reliving that time myself. The author's vivid, detailed, and honest storytelling brought back many memories I had lived through, and at times the emotions were so strong that brought me tears. It is not just a story to be read, but an experience to be felt and remembered. The details helped the reader truly understand what happened during the war in Kosovo and why it matters. I will highly recommend this book to everyone.

"Informative and interesting historical fiction."

One of the most informative and interesting historical fiction books I've read. The author was very knowledgeable of what the people of Kosovo experienced during the conflict in the Balkans. These details were beautifully blended into the lives of the fictional families in the book. The Historical Facts Timeline, Cast of Characters (fictional and historical) at the conclusion of the book were valuable tools to help review the order of all the events that took place.

"Well written and deeply researched."

A Murder of Crows Fly with a Red Ribbon is an important novel that I found to be well written and deeply researched. Based on firsthand accounts, the book follows a group of young people forced to flee their native Kosovo amid the brutal Serbian campaign against ethnic Albanians in the late 1990s, and who through remarkable courage, determination, and the goodness of others, find their way to the United States and a new life. I highly recommend it.

"A must read if you are visiting Eastern Europe!"

A well-researched historic novel set in Kosova. You feel like the characters were real people and the events depicted give great context for a trip to this region. I highly recommend it!

"A great read. Hard to put down."

I thoroughly enjoyed this book. I found it hard to put down. An extremely moving story based on true events. The book A Murder of Crows with a Red Ribbon is very well written. Learned so much about Kosovo and what people went through. Author was very knowledgeable of events. A book well worth reading.
