A Story of Courage & Survival

In the shadow of the Balkan Mountains, escalating ethnic tensions and brutal oppression in Kosova erupt into the NATO-led war against Serbia in 1999. This gripping novel follows two families—one Catholic, one Muslim—whose lives are torn apart by violence and upheaval.

As war and ethnic cleansing ravage their homeland, those who survive and flee to become refugees must confront an unimaginable cost: the loss of loved ones, homes, country, and at times, even their belief in God.
