A Story of Survival

A Murder of Crows Fly with a Red Ribbon is a historical novel set against the Kosova war of the late 1990s, following two intertwined stories of loss and survival. Ariana Hoxha, a young woman fleeing on false papers with nothing but a backpack and a red ribbon tied to her wrist, carries the weight of a family she may never see again.

Meanwhile, the Gashi brothers—one already building a new life in New York, one still in Kosova as the violence closes in—face the impossible choice of who leaves and who stays. Moving between the mountains of Kosova, the streets of Montenegro and Italy, and the unfamiliar sidewalks of New York, this novel traces what refugees carry, what they lose, and what it takes to begin again in a language and a country not their own.
